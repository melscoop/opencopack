Oklahoma co-packers in this directory

Updated 2026-11-19

Oklahoma was a priority for this directory because a competing public list had little coverage there. In this release we verified six plants from their own websites — not from a state agriculture co-packer spreadsheet, which we did not find.

See the living list on the Oklahoma hub. As of the seed build, the public pages we cited include Griffin Food Company in Muskogee (liquid and semi-solid private label, with SQF, OU Kosher, and USDA Organic mentioned on its private-label page), OWS Foods in Ponca City (a GFSI-certified sauce facility), Bakery Avenue in Tulsa (bakery and savory co-packing), Walke Brothers in Claremore (meat private label and co-packing), Firefly Brew in Tulsa (kombucha co-packing), and Focused Fluids in Oklahoma City (beverages, with a published minimum of 5,000 units).

None of those pages are a substitute for a call. We did not list companies whose public sites we could not load.

This is general information, not legal, food-safety, or business advice. Confirm process, labeling, and inspection rules with a process authority and the plant.
