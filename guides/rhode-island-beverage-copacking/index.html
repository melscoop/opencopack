Rhode Island beverage co-packing: what we verified

Updated 2026-11-20

Rhode Island is another state a competing directory had left thin. So far OpenCopack has one verified listing: Palate Pack in Lincoln, whose website describes beverage co-packing, canning, glass bottling, private-label programs for restaurant and hospitality groups, and ship-in repacking.

On minimums, the site says production programs are built around full runs. It did not publish a numeric MOQ in the text we captured, so our listing leaves the minimum blank rather than invent one.

If you run another Rhode Island plant that publicly offers contract packing, send a claim with the URL that says so. The state hub will update when we can verify it.

This is general information, not legal, food-safety, or business advice. Confirm process, labeling, and inspection rules with a process authority and the plant.
