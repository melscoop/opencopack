Why Wyoming and Washington, D.C. are empty on OpenCopack

We created state hubs for Wyoming and D.C. anyway. Here is why they have no listings yet.

OpenCopackPublished 2026-11-21Updated 2026-11-21

OpenCopack publishes a hub for Wyoming and for Washington, D.C. even though both currently have zero verified plants. That is deliberate. A competing directory had little or no coverage in those places, and an empty page is more honest than a guessed one.

In this research pass we looked at public pages that might have been contract packers. We did not add a company unless the page itself said it offers co-packing, private label, or contract manufacturing for other brands. A bottler that only describes its own brands, or a USDA organic handler that never mentions packing for others, is not enough.

If you operate a licensed plant in Wyoming or Washington, D.C. and your site already says you pack for other brands, please claim a listing.

This is general information, not legal, food-safety, or business advice. Confirm process, labeling, and inspection rules with a process authority and the plant.

OpenCopack compiles public lists. It is not a marketplace and does not endorse any plant. Confirm capabilities, minimums, certifications, and capacity directly with the company.

Co-packer listings are not paid placements. Some guides contain affiliate links; OpenCopack may earn a commission if you sign up through them.
