South Dakota co-packers in this directory

What Grand Prairie Foods in Sioux Falls publishes about private label and co-manufacturing, and what we have not yet verified elsewhere in the state.

OpenCopackPublished 2026-11-22Updated 2026-11-22

South Dakota currently has one verified listing on OpenCopack: Grand Prairie Foods in Sioux Falls. Its private-label page describes private label and co-manufacturing, in-house R&D, SQF Level 3, USDA inspection, FDA registration, and nut-free facilities, and it names product platforms such as breakfast sandwiches.

We attempted to load at least one other South Dakota company that is sometimes mentioned as a grain bagger; the public site did not load in this pass, so it is not listed. See the South Dakota hub for the current set.
