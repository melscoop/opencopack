Add or claim a plant

If you operate a licensed food plant that does contract manufacturing or private-label packing, you can ask to be listed or to correct an existing page. We will only publish capabilities, minimums, certifications, and contacts that already appear on a public page or that you send us in writing to post.

For now, open a GitHub issue on the OpenCopack repository with the details below. Nothing is billed, and there is no paid placement in this version of the site.

Open a GitHub issue to add or claim a plant

Please include

  • Plant name
  • City and state
  • A website URL that states you offer co-packing or private-label packing
  • Your name and role
  • What we should add or correct (published minimum, processes, certifications, address, or a new listing)

We may ask you to point us to a public page before a change goes live. Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive credentials in the issue.

OpenCopack compiles public lists. It is not a marketplace and does not endorse any plant. Confirm capabilities, minimums, certifications, and capacity directly with the company.

Co-packer listings are not paid placements. Some guides contain affiliate links; OpenCopack may earn a commission if you sign up through them.
