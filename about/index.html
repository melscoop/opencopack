About OpenCopack

OpenCopack is a small, public directory of U.S. food and beverage co-packers — plants that manufacture and package a recipe for another brand. It is built as a static site so it can be hosted on a free tier, and it is designed so one useful page can be added at a time.

The working principle is narrow: only publish what a public source already said, and show the source. We do not invent companies, minimum order quantities, certifications, or contact details. If a plant has not published a minimum, the listing says so.

What “verified” means here

It means we retrieved a public page — usually an official state agriculture directory or the company’s own site — and copied only what that page stated. It does not mean we toured the plant, confirmed open capacity, or tasted the product.

This first release

The seed directory was compiled on 2026-11-19 from the Colorado Department of Agriculture Co-Pack Directory, the Utah Department of Agriculture and Food Co-Packer Directory, a curated subset of the Minnesota Department of Agriculture Co-Packer Directory, and company websites for plants in Oklahoma, Rhode Island, and South Dakota. Wyoming and Washington, D.C. remain empty because we could not verify a public co-packing offer there in this pass.

OpenCopack is not affiliated with Co-Packer Match, PartnerSlate, Specialty Food Co-Packers, or any listed company.
