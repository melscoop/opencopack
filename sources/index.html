Methodology

Sources and disclaimer

Every plant page names the public source we used and the date we retrieved it. The 80 listings in this build come from:

  • Colorado Department of Agriculture Co-Pack Directory (Google Sheet linked from that page), retrieved 2026-11-19. We included rows marked “Co Packer” that had a city plus published product types or a minimum. Shared kitchens were left out.
  • Utah Department of Agriculture and Food Co-Packer Directory, retrieved 2026-11-19. The official table did not include business names; we added names only after reading them on the company’s own website. Two official rows were omitted because the website did not load or listed fulfillment only.
  • Minnesota Department of Agriculture Co-Packer Directory, retrieved 2026-11-19. This is a curated subset (sauces, beverages, snacks, dry mixes, and plants that published a minimum), not the entire MDA list.
  • Company websites for plants in Oklahoma, Rhode Island, and South Dakota, each cited on the plant page, retrieved 2026-11-19.

What we will not do

  • Invent a company, address, phone number, email, minimum, certification, or process.
  • Treat “organic handler” or a USDA inspection listing alone as proof the company offers co-packing.
  • Endorse a plant or guarantee open capacity.

Disclaimer

OpenCopack is an informational directory. Listings can go stale the day after we copy them. Food safety, labeling, cottage-food rules, and inspection requirements vary by product and state. Talk to the plant and, where required, a process authority before you produce anything. Links to plant and official-directory sites are provided for verification and are not paid placements. Some guides include affiliate links to tools; those are disclosed on the page, and OpenCopack may earn a commission if you sign up through them.

OpenCopack compiles public lists. It is not a marketplace and does not endorse any plant. Confirm capabilities, minimums, certifications, and capacity directly with the company.
